Privacy Policy — Good to Eat

Last updated: October 9, 2026

The Good to Eat app ("the App") lets you scan the barcode of a food product and get nutrition information and a score. We respect your privacy and collect only the minimum needed for the App to work. This policy explains what data is collected, why, and how it is stored.

What we collect

  • Photos you upload — when you choose to contribute a new product, the photos of the packaging you take are sent to our servers. The photos are re-encoded before sending, so location and other metadata (EXIF/GPS) are removed.
  • An anonymous device identifier — each installation gets a random identifier that contains neither your name, your email address, nor any other personal detail. It is used only to tell contributions from different devices apart and to prevent duplicates.
  • Personal account (optional) — you can sign in with Apple, with Google, or with an email address and password. In every case only the account's email address is stored (for signing in and resetting the password); we do not ask for your name. With Sign in with Apple you can choose "Hide My Email", in which case Apple's relay address is stored. With email sign-up the password is stored encrypted by our authentication provider (Supabase). The account keeps the list of products you marked as favorites (barcode, product name, image and score) so it appears on your other devices too. You can sign out or delete the account, together with its favorites, from inside the App (Favorites → Account → Delete account). Without an account, favorites are stored on the device only.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, phone number or any other personal detail. An email address is stored only if you chose to sign up with one.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites, and the App has no ads.
  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Camera permission

The App requests camera access only to scan barcodes and to photograph the packaging of new products. Photos are not saved or sent unless you explicitly choose to contribute a product.

How the data is stored

Photos and the anonymous identifier are stored in a secure cloud service (Supabase) that serves as the App's data infrastructure. Access is restricted and used only to operate the App and improve the product database.

Data sources

The product data and images shown in the App are gathered from public sources, including the price-transparency data of Israeli food retailers and the open Open Food Facts database. Open Food Facts data is distributed under the ODbL license and its images under CC-BY-SA.

Children

The App is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

For any privacy question, email us at goodtoeat.support@gmail.com

The information in the App is for general knowledge only and does not constitute medical or nutritional advice.
