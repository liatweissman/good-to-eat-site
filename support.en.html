Support — Good to Eat

Good to Eat scans a food product's barcode and shows a transparent 0–100 nutrition score, an explanation of the ingredients and additives, Israeli Ministry of Health "red label" warnings, and healthier alternatives. We're happy to help with any question, problem or suggestion.

Contact

Write to us at goodtoeat.support@gmail.com. We try to reply within a few business days.

Email support

What to include

  • Wrong or missing product — the barcode on the package (usually 13 digits) and the product name. A photo of the nutrition table and the ingredient list helps us fix it quickly.
  • A score that looks wrong — the barcode and the score shown. The method is explained inside the App under "How did we calculate the score?".
  • An app problem — your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what you did and what happened. A screenshot helps.

Frequently asked questions

  • The scan doesn't recognize the product. Make sure the barcode is well lit and fully inside the frame. You can also type the barcode manually. If the product isn't in the database, you can contribute it from inside the App.
  • The camera doesn't open. Check that camera permission is granted: Settings → Good to Eat → Camera.
  • Deleting your account. Inside the App: Favorites → Account → Delete account. The account and its favorites list are deleted.
